First Aid Swipe File

A quarterly refresher · Tap a card · Watch · Remember

This is a personal study tool, not a substitute for training. Take a real Red Cross course at least once. This page is what you open between courses, when you want a five-minute refresher on what to actually do.

Each card opens to a short list of steps and an embedded video from the Red Cross or another reputable source. Steps are summarized for memory; the video is the authoritative reference.

In an emergency

911

Call first if you can. Put the phone on speaker so you can keep your hands free. The dispatcher can talk you through CPR.
